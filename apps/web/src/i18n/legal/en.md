# Privacy Policy

LadeCompile is a free, no-login, sandboxed HTML/CSS/JS compiler. We practice local-first execution: your code is compressed into the URL hash or stored transiently in Cloudflare KV (30-day TTL) when you choose to share. We do not track telemetry, set no paywalled features, and store no account data.
